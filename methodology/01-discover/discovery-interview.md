# Discovery Interview Guide

> **Phase:** Discover
> **Purpose:** Extract the ground truth about a business process from the people who run it.
> **Instructions:** Run this as a 60-minute structured interview with a domain expert or team lead.

## Part 1: Understand the Current Workflow (15 min)

1. Walk me through a typical case from start to finish.
2. What information do you need to resolve it? Where do you find it?
3. What systems do you touch during a single case?
4. How long does a typical case take? What makes some take longer?
5. What's the most common mistake or misjudgment you see?

**Notes:**

---

## Part 2: Identify High-Cost Moments (15 min)

6. Which step in the process is the most time-consuming?
7. Which step requires the most expertise or experience?
8. Which step do new team members get wrong most often?
9. Where do escalations usually originate?
10. Where do you feel most uncertain when making a decision?

**Notes:**

---

## Part 3: Probe Risk and Constraints (15 min)

11. What would happen if the wrong decision was made here?
12. Are there compliance or regulatory constraints I should know about?
13. Which actions require approval from a senior or manager?
14. Are there any actions that should never be automated?
15. What data is sensitive or access-restricted?

**Notes:**

---

## Part 4: Define Success (15 min)

16. If this worked perfectly, what would change for you day to day?
17. How would you know if an AI decision was correct vs. incorrect?
18. What would make you trust the system enough to rely on it?
19. What would make you stop using it immediately?
20. Who else should I talk to before building anything?
