# Example: Charged After Cancellation

> **Status:** Planned
> **Business problem:** A customer was charged after cancelling their subscription.
> **Demonstrates:** Method transferability across industries; CRM + billing + subscription system integration; SaaS scenario.

---

## Business Context

A customer contacts support reporting an unexpected charge after cancelling their subscription. The agent must:

1. Verify the cancellation was processed and when
2. Check the billing system for the disputed charge
3. Determine whether the charge is valid (e.g., within billing cycle) or erroneous
4. Provide a clear explanation or initiate a refund workflow
